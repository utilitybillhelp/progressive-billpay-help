## Project purpose

This repository and the website it builds provide neutral, practical guidance about insurer and utility bill payment processes. Our primary goals are:

- Explain how and where people can pay insurance and utility bills.
- Describe common payment methods and timelines.
- Help users navigate billing support channels and self‑service options.
- Remain non‑commercial and informational — we do not process payments or collect personal data.

---

## Editorial policy

- Audience: consumers seeking clear, non‑technical explanations of payment processes and options.
- Tone: factual, neutral, and accessible. Avoid promotional language.
- Scope: general guidance and links to official insurer resources; do not give legal, tax, or financial advice.
- Personal data: we do not publish or collect personal financial or account information.
- Phone numbers and contact details are shown only as examples or when citing official source pages.

## Sources & research

Content is compiled from:

## Publishing & branch workflow

- Changes should be made on a named branch with a descriptive name (e.g., `edit/humans-update` or `fix/contact-link`).

- Editorial changes are reviewed by at least one maintainer; substantive policy changes require two reviewers when possible.

## Contribution & editorial process

We welcome corrections and suggested improvements.

How to contribute:

1. Search existing issues to avoid duplicates.
2. Open an issue describing the proposed change (include source links and rationale).
3. Optionally, open a Pull Request with the proposed edit. Use a clear PR title and reference the issue.
4. Maintain editorial clarity: prefer concise explanations, use accessible language, and cite primary sources.

Editorial style highlights:

- Use plain language and short paragraphs.
- Use bullets for steps and processes.
- Prefer official links and avoid screenshots of private pages.
