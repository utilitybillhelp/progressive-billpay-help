Which Progressive policies can be paid as a guest?

Most retail policies support guest payments. Below are common types and any special notes you should know before paying as a guest.

Auto insurance

Auto policies are the most common guest-payment use case. You’ll need the policy number and policyholder last name. If the vehicle has multiple drivers or additional coverages, confirm the displayed address and coverage dates before paying.

Motorcycle insurance

Motorcycle policies follow the same guest flow as auto. Ensure the policy number matches the bike owner and have VIN info handy if asked for verification.

RV, boat, and specialty vehicle policies

RV and other specialty coverages usually accept guest payments. However, if your policy includes seasonal coverage (e.g., winter storage), confirm the effective dates displayed on the policy screen before submitting.

Commercial & business policies

Some commercial or business policies may require an account or additional verification. If the guest flow does not find the policy, contact billing support for business account procedures or invoicing options.

Accepted payment methods & what to expect

Credit & debit cards

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover are typically accepted. Card payments usually post in 1–2 business days. Some card payments may include a convenience fee — the guest payment page will display fees before you confirm.

Bank account (ACH)

ACH transfers (routing + account) are commonly accepted for guest payments, and they tend to be the most economical (often no fee). ACH processing typically takes 1–3 business days to post.

Digital wallets & quick pay

Depending on Progressive’s current integrations, Apple Pay or Google Pay may be available and can be the fastest option (same-day processing in many cases).

Pay by phone

If you prefer or need human assistance, you can pay by phone. See the phone payment instructions below for what to expect and the number commonly cited in online resources.

Other methods

Some regions or policy types may allow mailed checks or in-branch payments. Mailing addresses or local office options are usually listed on billing statements or the official Progressive help pages.

Step-by-step — make a guest / one-time payment (detailed)

Step 1 — Prepare (2–3 minutes)

  • Have your policy number, policy holder last name, billing ZIP or DOB ready.
  • Decide payment method (card or ACH) and have details ready to enter.
  • Keep an email address ready to receive the receipt.

Step 2 — Confirm official site and open guest pay (1 minute)

Type progressive.com/pay-bill directly into your browser. Avoid clicking links in unsolicited email or text messages. Look for the padlock (secure) and progressive.com in the address bar.

Step 3 — Enter verification and choose amount (3–5 minutes)

  • Enter policy number and last name exactly as printed (watch for leading zeros).
  • Provide ZIP or DOB when requested; if one fails, try the other format.
  • Choose full balance or partial payment if partial is permitted.

Step 4 — Enter payment and confirm (2–3 minutes)

  • Enter card or ACH details; verify billing ZIP and CVV for cards.
  • Review any convenience fee and the processing time before confirming.
  • Submit payment and wait for the confirmation screen; screenshot and save the confirmation number and receipt email.

Step 5 — What to do after payment

  • Save confirmation number and email for 6 months.
  • Allow the typical posting window (cards 1–2 days, ACH 1–3 days) before contacting support about posting issues.

One-time payment by phone — exact script & expectations

If you cannot pay online or want help, call the billing line and request a one-time guest payment. When you call: clearly state you need a one-time guest payment, have your policy number ready, and be prepared to provide the payment method.

Phone guidance: If you prefer to speak with someone, many online guides and discussion posts reference the billing assistance number 1-888-414-4406 — ask the representative for a confirmation number and the expected posting time. If a fee or hold is mentioned, ask whether an alternate payment method (ACH) would avoid the fee.

Quick answers (bullet-shot) — instant solutions

  • Can I pay without an account? Yes — use the guest/quick-pay at progressive.com/pay-bill.
  • How fast will it post? Card: 1–2 business days. ACH: 1–3 business days. Digital wallet: often same day.
  • Payment declined? Re-enter details, try another method, clear autofill, or call support with the confirmation number.
  • Paying someone else? Yes, if you have their policy number and verification details; confirm the policy holder approved the payment.
  • Fees? Card convenience fees sometimes apply; ACH and debit usually do not. Fees are shown before confirmation.

Troubleshooting & instant fixes

Auth & verification errors

  • Confirm exact policy number and last name (including punctuation and leading zeros).
  • Try billing ZIP versus DOB formats (ZIP first, then DOB MM/DD/YYYY).

Declines and payment errors

  • Clear browser cache or use an incognito window to remove bad autofill values.
  • Try a different card or ACH. Contact the card issuer for holds or fraud flags.

Posting delays

  • Wait the expected window, then use your confirmation number when contacting support.
  • If the posted date is critical (e.g., to avoid lapse), call support immediately and ask for expedited posting options.

Refunds, disputes and next steps

If a payment posts incorrectly or you’re charged in error, gather your confirmation number and receipt, contact Progressive billing first, and if unresolved, open a dispute with your card issuer. Keep dated screenshots and any representative names/emails to speed resolution.
