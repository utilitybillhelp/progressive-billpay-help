Step-by-step: Make a one-time (guest) payment online

  1. Open Progressive's official payment page: Visit https://www.progressive.com/pay-bill/. Confirm the address bar shows progressive.com and a padlock icon.
  2. Choose guest/quick pay: Click “Pay as Guest” or “Quick Pay.” This path does not require an account login.
  3. Enter verification details: Provide the policy number, the policy holder's last name, and either the billing ZIP code or DOB when prompted. These fields let Progressive match the policy record.
  4. Review the displayed balance: Confirm the amount shown is correct; choose full payment or a partial amount if the option appears.
  5. Select payment method: Enter card details (credit/debit) or bank routing + account (ACH). Check processing times and any convenience fees before confirming.
  6. Confirm and save confirmation: Review, submit, and save the confirmation number shown on screen. Expect an email receipt if you provided an email address. For phone assistance instead, call 1-888-414-4406.

Make a one-time payment by phone (summary)

  1. Call the support line: Dial 1-888-414-4406 and follow menu prompts for billing or guest payment, or request a representative for a one-time payment.
  2. Verify identity: Provide the policy number and policy holder last name. The representative will confirm the payment amount.
  3. Provide payment details: Give card or bank account details using the secure phone prompts; request a confirmation number and estimated posting time.
Security tip: Only enter payment details on progressive.com or give them by phone to a verified representative. Do not follow unsolicited links or provide payment details via text or unverified email.

Quick checklist — what to have ready

  • Policy number (found on ID card or bill)
  • Policy holder last name
  • Billing ZIP code or date of birth
  • Payment method: card number/CVV or bank routing + account for ACH
  • Email address to receive a receipt (optional but recommended)

Frequently asked — PAA-ready answers

Can I pay a Progressive bill without logging in?
Yes. Use Progressive’s guest/quick-pay at progressive.com/pay-bill or call 1-888-414-4406 for phone assistance to complete a one-time payment.
How long does a guest payment take to post?
Processing varies: debit/digital wallets can post same day, credit cards typically 1–2 business days, and ACH 1–3 business days.
Can I pay someone else’s Progressive bill as a guest?
Yes, if you have their policy number and required verification details you can make a guest payment on their behalf.
